## NASA DATA & RESOURCES
- **PSI Data Repository: Combustion Science** — Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) source of the data upon which the NASA Space App is to be based. https://psi.nasa.gov/physci/repo/search?q=combustion
- **NASA Combustion Science** — Basic information about most of the combustion investigations for which data is found in the PSI Data Repository. https://www.nasa.gov/glenn/glenn-expertise-space-exploration/physical-sciences-program/combustion-science/
- **NASA ScienceCasts: The Power of Studying Combustion on the ISS** — 3.6-minute video clip from 2018. https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W1oXykoz9zg
- **A Researcher’s Guide to International Space Station Combustion Science** — A 2015 review of NASA’s combustion research conducted on, or planned for, the ISS. Basic information about most of the investigations described in this 50+ page document is found within the PSI Data Repository. https://www.nasa.gov/missions/station/a-researchers-guide-to-combustion-science/
- **Apollo 1** — crew mission, pre-launch-test fire that killed the crew, investigation, lessons learned. https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-1
- **Apollo 13** — crew, mission, oxygen tank explosion en route to the Moon, investigation, lessons learned. https://sma.nasa.gov/sma-disciplines/accllp/apollo-13
- **25 Years Ago: Fire Aboard Space Station Mir** — fire caused by an oxygen generator failure aboard Mir in 1997 during joint operations preparing for the ISS (first occupied 2000). https://www.nasa.gov/history/25-years-ago-fire-aboard-space-station-mir/
- **Fireman’s Air Tanks** — 1970s adaptation of spacesuit technology to improve firefighters’ air tanks and breathing gear. https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9582
- **Fire Detector** — 1970s smoke detector developed for the shuttle; discriminates smoke from dust, fewer nuisance alarms; industrial uses (e.g., mining). https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9407
- **New Technology for the Fire Attire** — 1980s application of NASA spacesuit technology to improve firefighter clothing. https://spinoff.nasa.gov/node/9918
