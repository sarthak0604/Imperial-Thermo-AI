# Participant FAQs (dated November 13, 2026)
Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/participant-faqs/ (scraped 14 Nov 2026; transcribed)

## About the challenge
- **Dates:** 14–15 November 2026.
- **Theme:** "The Next Frontier" — from your own backyard to the edges of the universe; collaborate to turn data into tools that benefit humanity and propel us to the Moon, Mars and beyond.
- **Who:** students, technologists, space science enthusiasts; any age, skill level or professional background.
- **Cost:** free. If asked to pay for any aspect, do not pay; report to info@spaceappschallenge.org.
- **Under 18:** open to all ages; must be registered by a parent/legal guardian and accompanied at all times during the event; Local Events may add requirements. Under-18s must not upload their own photo as Avatar/Team page photo, nor use photo/video/voice/likeness of themselves or others under 18 in the demo or submission.
- **Where:** hundreds of in-person or virtual Local Events (run by Local Leads) or the virtual Universal Event.
- **Conduct concerns:** Local Lead and/or conduct@spaceappschallenge.org (GO Team may warn or expel).
- Participant Terms & Conditions: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/legal/

## Important dates
- Aug 26: registration opens · Sep 17: challenge summaries + team formation open, Team Formation Guide · Oct 28: challenge statements · Nov 3: Space Apps Connect opens + guide · Nov 13: Global Offers, challenge resources, Project Submission and Judging & Awards guides · Nov 14–15: event.
- After: judging by experts from NASA, Space Agency Partners and industry. Dec 2026: Global Nominees, Finalists, Honorable Mentions. Jan 2027: Global Winners.

## How to participate / register
1. Log in or create an account (new accounts verify email).
2. Register for 2026 (opened Aug 26; open through end of hackathon Nov 15); complete required questions and read T&Cs.
3. Choose a Local Event (waitlist if at capacity; "Leave this Event" to change) or the Universal Event.
4. Connect with your Local Lead (contact info on Local Event page). GO Team and Local Leads send updates via Space Apps Messages (profile menu).
5. Form/join a team (opens Sep 17); join your Local Event Nov 14–15; submit by your Local Event's end time.

## Universal Event
Virtual and asynchronous, Nov 14–15. Teams form like Local Events. Projects judged in the same period but only against other Universal submissions; Global Nominee selection rate comparable to Local Events. Local Events are encouraged (community, coaching, in-person). Universal team minimum: 2 participants. Use Space Apps Connect "Find a Team" from Nov 3.

## Guides
Team Formation Guide (Sep 17), Space Apps Connect Guide (Nov 3), Project Submission Guide (Nov 13), Judging and Awards Guide (Nov 13). Questions: Contact Us page.

## Challenges
Authored by NASA and Space Agency Partner SMEs; summaries Sep 17, statements Oct 28 (full details/requirements), resources Nov 13. **Teams are not allowed to begin working on the challenges prior to the hackathon.**

## Teams
Required. Local Event teams ≥1 (global-judging eligibility); Universal ≥2; max 6; all signed up for the same Local Event or the Universal Event. Only one team per person; one challenge per team. Recommended to team up with people of varied skills.

## Submission
Via the team's project page on spaceappschallenge.org (instructions in Project Submission Guide).

## Super Intelligence (SI)
Per the Sept 29, 2026 U.S. Executive Order, "the executive branch shall use the terms 'Super Intelligence' and 'SI' in place of 'Artificial Intelligence' and 'AI.'" SI = computer systems performing tasks typically associated with human reasoning, decision-making, content creation and problem-solving (LLMs, conversational assistants, image/video generation, foundation models, code generation). Participants may use SI tools to accelerate innovation, streamline development and enhance creativity. Each submission must include a description of how SI tools were used.

## Space Apps Connect
Free collaboration forum for registered participants (Local Leads, Navigators, SMEs, Global Collaborators, GO Team). Optional but encouraged. Opens Nov 3, closes at end of hackathon. (Forum: https://github.com/nasa/spaceapps)

## Recognition and awards
All participants who submit get a profile certificate. Judged on criteria for Honorable Mentions, Global Finalists, Global Winners; winners invited to a virtual Winner's Celebration early 2027.
