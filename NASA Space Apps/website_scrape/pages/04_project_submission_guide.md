# Project Submission Guide (dated November 13, 2026)
Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/project-submission-guide/ (scraped 14 Nov 2026; transcribed)

## Project submission start & end times
- Opens Saturday, Nov 14 at 9:00 a.m. in the time zone of your team's Local Event; closes Sunday, Nov 15 at 11:59 p.m. in that time zone.
- Exception: Universal Event teams — opens/closes per the local time of the Team Owner's area of residence.
- Check with your Local Lead: local judging/awards times may differ from global submission times.

## Editing your project submission page
This page is what judges review; the more information, the better judges can rate it.
- Access: profile icon (top right) → "My Team" → "Project" tab (appears only when the hackathon begins Nov 14, 2026, 9:00 a.m. local).
- Edit: blue "Edit" button; save often with blue "Save Progress" (changes only visible to your team until submitted). Toggle the edit "Lock" ON to signal you're editing — but another member can still remove the lock and overwrite unpublished changes.
- Files: page allows upload/paste of images (JPG, PNG, GIF up to 10 MB), NOT videos or documents. Host those on a cloud service/repo (YouTube, Google Drive, GitHub, OneDrive, Dropbox…) with a public link needing no logins/permissions/registration. Set to public and test in a private browser or with teammates. If judges can't access it, it can't be judged. You may link a slide deck, video or other visual aids (see Global Collaborators page).
- New website? Request a FortiGuard Web Filter Classification (optional): fill the form, choose the category best describing your site (education, personal website/blog, reference, web-based application…), Company Name "N/A", submit. Categorization is at FortiGuard's discretion.

## Project page form fields
1. **Project Name**
2. **High-Level Project Summary** — what did you develop, how does it address the challenge, why is it important?
3. **Link to Project Demo** — 7-slide presentation (incl. title/intro slides) or ≤30-second video, hosted externally, publicly accessible, no password/permission/registration. Demo is considered in overall evaluation but most is based on the project submission; a strong demo can raise Presentation score.
4. **Link to Final Project** — website, cloud service, repo, YouTube/Drive/GitHub/OneDrive/Dropbox etc.; no password/permission/registration.
5. **Detailed Project Description** — What does it do? How does it work? Benefits? What do you hope to achieve? Tools, languages, hardware, software used?
6. **Use of Super Intelligence (SI)** — Did you use SI tools/software? How? SI tools (machine learning, chatbots, image generation, LLMs) are permitted to accelerate innovation and creativity. "SI should not be used to (come up with your idea of how to solve the challenge)." Generated content may not use, contain, or modify NASA branding or design elements (logos, flags, mission identifiers). Clearly indicate where/how SI was used: Images and videos — visible watermark indicating SI generation; Audio — acknowledge SI generation in descriptive text and metadata; Code and data — acknowledge SI generation in descriptive text and metadata. The SI question is for data collection only and not seen by judges; judges see SI-generated indicators in the project (watermarks, descriptive text, metadata) and evaluate originality, intent and execution of SI-assisted elements.
7. **NASA Data** — specifics of NASA data/resources used. To be eligible for a Global Award you must use at least one NASA data or resource.
8. **Space Agency Partner Data and Other References** — list all data, resources and tools used, including any code, text, images (even open source/freely available); get permission for copyrighted materials. Highly encouraged to use data/resources from 2026 Space Agency Partners: Angola National Space Programme Management Office, Bahrain Space Agency, Brazilian Space Agency, CSA, ESA, ISRO, Italian Space Agency, JAXA, KASA (Korea AeroSpace Administration), Mohammed Bin Rashid Space Center (UAE), National Space Activities Commission of Argentina, Nigeria's NASRDA, Paraguayan Space Agency, Senegalese Space Studies Agency, Spanish Space Agency, Turkish Space Agency, UK Space Agency.

## Tips for creating a project demo
- All teams must create a demo: high-level summary + demonstration of the final project (incl. any software/hardware).
- Format: video ≤30 s OR slide deck ≤7 slides (incl. title and intro slides). Host externally (can't upload to the site).
- Language: English on submission page and linked materials. Video may be spoken in another language only with English subtitles. Slide decks must be English.
- Judged on five criteria: Impact, Creativity, Validity, Relevance, Presentation.
- Plan ahead (choose video or deck; "What story does my team want to tell?"), delegate a demo lead, tell a visual story for someone with no context; strong visuals, readability.
- Video timeline: [0:00] introduce yourself (name/team); [0:02] lead us in — which challenge, what inspired you; [0:05] project title and tagline; [0:10] describe your idea — how it works, images/prototype, user experience, how NASA and Space Agency Partner open data made it possible; [0:20–0:30] look into the future — what could it do for people, the world, and beyond.
- Slide deck: ≤7 slides; readable template/font, few words; visuals should display an image of the project, describe the user experience, include how NASA/space agency open data and technology make the solution possible; content must include team name, names of team members, challenge chosen.
- Exceeding 30 s / 7 slides = not eligible for Global Judging.
- Past Global Winner demo examples: click "Project Tab" then scroll to the Demo section on previous winners' Project pages.

## Submitting your project
Requirements for judging:
- All team members must be confirmed participants at the same Local Event (if waitlisted, ask the Local Lead; if declined/no response, every member should switch to another Local Event with room for all).
- All required fields completed; all content in English.
- Click "Submit for Judging" before Sunday Nov 15, 11:59 p.m. local time of your Local Event (Universal: Team Owner's local time). Anything saved or submitted after the deadline is ineligible.
- Demos and final projects may not include images, video, audio or other likeness of persons under 18 (Terms & Conditions).
Steps: tick "Terms and Conditions" box; tick "Confirm All Work is Original" box; click "Submit for Judging" (can't submit if required sections incomplete). A banner and confirmation message appear on success. You can edit and resubmit unlimited times until the end of the hackathon; only your last saved submission is reviewed.

## Participant certificate
- Available to participants who submit a project (before the deadline) — automatically on your Profile once submitted.
- Profile icon → "Profile" → "Participant Information" → "2026 NASA Space Apps Challenge" → yellow "Download Participant Certificate" button. Select paper size "Letter" (8.5x11in / 215x297mm [sic]); PDF only; use laptop/desktop.
- Technical issues: web@spaceappschallenge.org with username and team name.
- Name on certificate = "Full Name" in Profile (Account Information → Change); can change anytime, even after submission; follow NASA Space Apps Values.
