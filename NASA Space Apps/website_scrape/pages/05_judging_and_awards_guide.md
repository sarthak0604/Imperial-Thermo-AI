# Judging & Awards Guide (dated November 13, 2026)
Source: https://www.spaceappschallenge.org/resources/judging-awards-guide/ (scraped 14 Nov 2026; transcribed)

## Purpose
At its core NASA Space Apps is a collaborative event, not a competitive one. All participants who submit a project by the deadline receive a certificate of participation.

## Eligibility for Global Judging (minimum requirements)
- Respond to one official NASA Space Apps Challenge.
- All team members registered, same Local Event (not waitlisted) or Universal Event, and listed on the team's Members tab. No new members after submission closes. Local Event teams ≥1 person; Universal Event teams ≥2; max 6. All teammates incl. Team Owner must be registered for the same Local Event.
- Complete all required fields on the Project Submission page.
- Provide a demo: slide presentation (7-slide limit incl. title/intro) or video (30-second limit).
- English on the submission page and linked materials (video may be spoken in another language with English subtitles).
- Submit via Project tab → Edit → Submit before Sunday Nov 15, 11:59 p.m. local time of your Local Event (Universal: Team Owner's local time). Ask your Local Lead about deadline questions.
- Tick: "I have read and understand the project submission requirements as contained in the NASA Space Apps Project Submission Guide, and I agree to the Participant Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy."
- Tick: "I confirm that the submitted project represents my team’s original work and that all external resources including code, text and images (even if they are open source or freely available) used in the project are listed in the References field of the project submission form. In creating our solution, my team has not used any copyrighted materials (i.e., music, images, text, etc.) that we don’t have permission to use."
- China funding restriction: teams undergo review for prohibited bilateral activity with China or a Chinese-owned entity (any department/agency/instrumentality of the government of China incl. subdivisions, Hong Kong and Macau; any company owned by China; any company, university or similar institution incorporated under Chinese law). Eligibility determined by NASA in its sole discretion based on information provided by the applicant. See Terms & Conditions for impact on Global Winner recognition.

## Judging criteria (scored 1–5 at every level, Local/Universal through Executive)
- **Impact** — addresses a minor or major problem; quality and quantity of overall effect; inspires/helps a broad audience or select few. Score 5 (Outstanding): exceptional impact, significant problem with proposed solutions, extensive reach, inspiring, large audience or could change the world; quality/effectiveness far exceeds expectations.
- **Creativity** — originality and innovation; novel concept not attempted before or a meaningful improvement on existing ideas; fresh perspective or inventive execution. Score 5: remarkable creativity, groundbreaking approach never attempted before, challenges conventional methods, original and inventive, a true innovation.
- **Validity** — solution grounded in scientific principles; technical, functional or logical feasibility; effective for its purpose; potential real-world function; may be fully developed or need refinement; usability/user-friendliness matter. Score 5: scientifically sound and technically feasible, strong alignment with established principles, fully developed and ready for real-world implementation, highly usable/user-friendly, accessible and practical for widespread application.
- **Relevance** — fully and directly addresses the challenge; meaningfully integrates NASA data as a core component, not superficial; depth of engagement with data enhances credibility. Score 5: thoroughly engages the challenge, deeply connected to goals, NASA data integral to success, providing meaningful insights; well-executed use of data enhancing credibility and impact.
- **Presentation** — effectively communicated challenge, solution and significance; engaging, well-structured storytelling; clarity, organization and delivery. Judges evaluate the demo AND full project. Score 5: well-structured, compelling presentation, polished, highly effective.
Guide advice: pay equal attention to presentation; devote time to the pitch and story; recruit teammates with storytelling and design skills.

## Phase 1: Local Event judging & Global Nominees
- Local judges (recruited/trained/organized by Local Lead) pick projects to advance. Local awards are optional and separate from Global Judging. Only officially submitted Global Nominees go to Global Judging.
- Universal Event: evaluated by subject matter experts; number advancing proportional to Local Event advancement.
- Global Nominees announced **December 3, 2026**.

## Phase 2: Global Finalists & Honorable Mentions
- Expert judges review all Global Nominees against the criteria; votes determine Global Finalists. Announced **December 17, 2026**, with Honorable Mentions (ranked highly for ingenuity/creativity but not advancing to Executive Judging or eligible for awards).

## Phase 3: Global Winners
Executive committee from NASA and 2026 Space Agency Partners (agency leaders/executives) evaluates each Global Finalist and nominates teams for ten Global Awards:
- Best Use of Science: the project that makes the best and most valid use of the scientific method.
- Best Use of Data: best makes space data accessible or leverages it to a unique application.
- Best Use of Technology: the most innovative use of technology.
- Galactic Impact: most potential to improve life on Earth or the universe.
- Best Mission Concept: most plausible concept and design.
- Most Inspirational: the project that captures our hearts.
- Best Storytelling Award: most creatively communicates the potential of open data through the art of storytelling.
- Global Connection Award: best connects people around the world through technology.
- Art & Technology Award: most effectively combines technical and creative skills.
- Local Impact Award: greatest potential for making a difference in their local community.
Global Winners announced **January 26, 2027**; invited to a virtual Winner's Celebration in early 2027.
