Model widgets — preview

Built from the thermo team's models in Imperial-Thermo-AI, run by standin/precompute_models.py. Not on the main site yet. Badges show what each one is: a model calculation, a reconstruction of a real NASA test, a model estimate, or a synthetic demo.

THM-02 · Gas transport & Lewis number

Kinetic-theory calculation from the inputs — no fake data. 459 pre-computed mixtures: 3 fuels × 3 diluents × 3 cabin pressures × O₂ 15–95%, at 300 K.

THM-11 · Two-stage droplet burning

Real NASA FLEX evidence (n-decane, 0.5 atm). R377 is reproduced from its own data; R379 is an estimate whose cool-flame end was held out and checked.

THM-16 · Time to ignition of a solid

Physics model with a SYNTHETIC demo material (4 mm slab) — the thermo team marked it illustrative. Only calibrated case: 101.3 kPa, 21% O₂, 20 cm/s airflow.

THM-04 · Heat release from oxygen use

Reduces a measured NIST burner-test record to heat release rate and total energy. Ground-based reference test, not a space run; the burner's own meter is drawn for comparison.

THM-05 · Opposed-flow flame spread

Thermally-thin spread speed against sheet thickness, checked against two measured points.

THM-06 · Concurrent flame spread

ILLUSTRATIVE inputs: the growth curve shows the model's shape, not a measured flame.

THM-15 · Soot inception

Screening estimate with ILLUSTRATIVE inputs: local flame temperature against the soot-inception threshold.

THM-17 · Limiting oxygen against airflow

Estimate of the lowest oxygen level that still burns at each airflow speed, between quench and blow-off limits.

THM-18 · Suppression thermal budget

ILLUSTRATIVE inputs: heat accounting for CO₂ dilution and a small water-mist dose.

THM-19 · Cabin pressure after a fire

ILLUSTRATIVE inputs: sealed-cabin pressure and temperature rise from a heat-release curve.

THM-20 · Smoke ageing in a duct

ILLUSTRATIVE inputs: particle count and size drifting along a duct to a detector.

THM-01 · Adiabatic flame temperature

Equilibrium calculation: the hottest a flame can get in ISS, lunar habitat and spacesuit air. A thermodynamic ceiling; real flames lose heat.

THM-03 · Flame position (Burke-Schumann)

Idealised infinitely fast chemistry: where a methane flame sphere sits around a small burner as the oxygen supplied changes.

THM-07 · Damköhler blow-off

Single-step reaction with generic constants: how ventilation speed pushes a flame towards blow-off.

THM-08 · Buoyancy vs forced airflow

Moon and Mars values are theoretical scaling bounds: PSI has no partial-gravity combustion data.

THM-09 · Spalding B-number

Mass-transfer driving force and burn rate for PMMA against oxygen level.
